yt-conveyor uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the operator agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Personal data is handled as described in the yt-conveyor Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool does

It uploads the operator's own videos to the operator's own YouTube channels as private videos with a scheduled publication time, marks them as altered or synthetic content, and reads back their upload status. It does not access other users' channels or data.

The operator is responsible for every uploaded video complying with YouTube Community Guidelines and policies, including disclosure of AI-generated content.
