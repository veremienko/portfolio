yt-conveyor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

yt-conveyor is a private, single-user desktop tool operated by Andrii Veremiienko (independent developer, Ukraine) on his own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool accesses

With the owner's consent via Google OAuth, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels, set their thumbnails, schedule their publication time, and read back their upload status. Scopes used: youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.

What is stored

The OAuth token and the video metadata are stored only on the owner's computer. No YouTube data of other users is collected, stored, shared or sold. No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. Data goes only to Google/YouTube as part of the upload itself.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions; the local token is then deleted.

Terms

See also the yt-conveyor Terms of Use.

Google policies

The tool uses YouTube API Services and complies with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
